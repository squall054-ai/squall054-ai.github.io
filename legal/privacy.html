Spell Surge Privacy Policy

Spell Surge ("the Game") is developed and published by FatyFlyns Studios.

Website:

Support:
support_ss@fatyflyns.com

Introduction

Your privacy is important to us.

Spell Surge has been designed to provide an immersive Mixed Reality multiplayer experience while minimizing the collection of personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game uses, how it is processed, and what data is (and is not) collected.

Information We Do Not Collect

Spell Surge does not collect or store:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your contacts
  • Your photos
  • Your videos
  • Your documents
  • Your precise location
  • Your conversations
  • Your biometric information

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Camera Access

Spell Surge requests camera access exclusively to enable Mixed Reality features provided by Meta Quest.

The camera is used for:

  • Passthrough visualization
  • AprilTag detection
  • Shared spatial alignment between players

Camera images are processed locally by the headset. Spell Surge does not record, store, transmit, or upload camera images.

Microphone Access

Spell Surge requests microphone permission to recognize spoken spell commands.

Voice recognition is performed locally on the headset using the Vosk Speech Recognition engine.

  • Audio recordings are not stored.
  • Voice data is not transmitted to external servers.
  • Voice data is not used for advertising or profiling.

Local Data Storage

Spell Surge stores limited information locally on your headset, including:

  • Initial setup completion
  • Local preferences
  • Configuration settings

This information never leaves your device.

Multiplayer

Spell Surge supports Local Area Network (LAN) multiplayer.

Network communication is used exclusively to synchronize gameplay between players.

No gameplay data is stored on FatyFlyns servers.

Internet Usage

Spell Surge does not require an Internet connection to play LAN matches.

An Internet connection may be required for:

  • Downloading the game
  • Game updates
  • Meta platform services
  • Operating system updates

Analytics

Spell Surge currently does not include analytics services. No gameplay statistics are collected by FatyFlyns.

Advertising

Spell Surge contains no advertisements. No advertising identifiers are collected.

In-App Purchases

Spell Surge currently offers no in-app purchases.

Children

Spell Surge is intended for players aged 10 years and older. Parents or guardians should supervise younger players when using Mixed Reality devices.

Third-Party Software

Spell Surge uses third-party software components, including:

  • Unity Engine
  • Meta XR SDK
  • Netcode for GameObjects
  • Unity Transport
  • Vosk Speech Recognition

Each component remains subject to its own license.

Security

Because Spell Surge stores only minimal local configuration data and does not operate backend servers, the amount of personal information processed is intentionally minimized.

Changes to this Policy
