SpellSurge Privacy Notice

Updated: October 27, 2026.

Controller and contact

Alejandro Ivan Ontiveros Moreno, trading as FatyFlyns realidad virtual, is responsible for the processing performed for SpellSurge. Address: Fray Antonio San Miguel 1920, colonia Periodistas, Morelia, Michoacán, Mexico. Support and privacy: support_ss@fatyflyns.com.

This notice covers the game, its PC/Android controllers and support. Independent event organizers may control their own participant lists, imports and copies; request their notice as well.

Data and purposes

We process information needed to run matches, recognize spells, align shared spaces, manage maps/tournaments, troubleshoot and handle privacy rights. Operators may enter aliases or names, teams, headset labels, results and logo images. Prefer aliases and avoid sensitive information in room names, imported files and support messages.

The microphone enables local Vosk spell recognition. The inspected flow does not send audio to a transcription service or provide a gameplay voice-recording feature. Noiz was used to create sound assets; SpellSurge does not send players' voices to Noiz.

Camera, depth, head/hand/torso tracking and spatial data support mixed reality, AprilTag detection, avatars, occlusion and colocation. The inspected application code does not upload photos or video to our own server. Meta processes data required for headset features and shared spatial anchors. We do not use this data to biometrically identify individuals. The game does not request health or financial information or identity documents; privacy-rights requests may require proportionate identity or representative verification.

Connections use network addresses, technical identifiers and game state. Support receives your email address, message and files you choose to send. The reviewed configuration does not enable Unity Ads, Analytics, in-app purchases or automatic Unity crash reports. This does not exclude technical records maintained by providers or the headset system. We do not use support data for advertising or sell it.

External services and disclosures

Unity Services mode uses Unity Authentication, Lobby/Sessions and Distributed Authority. Anonymous authentication creates an identifier; it does not mean that no personal data exists. Unity receives connection/session information to connect and synchronize players. LAN mode exchanges information between devices; selecting LAN does not itself disable Meta services.

Meta provides the platform, distribution and spatial features. Shared anchors may transfer spatial data to Meta. Destroying a scene object does not guarantee deletion of a remote anchor. Purchases are handled by the store; the game does not request payment-card details.

Support email and the website use hosting providers. These services and Unity/Meta may process information outside Mexico under their applicable terms and mechanisms. We do not promise one hosting region. We disclose information for the functions described, rights requests or legal obligations; consent must be obtained before processing when required.

See https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/. Provider policies supplement this information and do not replace our responsibilities. The public website uses GitHub Pages: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

Retention

  • Local maps, tournaments and recovery copies: until deleted. Saving may replace a .bak copy; synchronization may retain tournament-before-sync.json. These do not expire automatically. Headset labels are stored separately.
  • Internal console: up to 250 in-memory entries, reset at startup. Unity/operating-system logs are managed separately.
  • Diagnostics and attachments received by support: deleted 30 calendar days after the incident closes, including copies under our control. A summary without attachments may be retained with correspondence.
  • Support correspondence: 12 months after closure, except documented legal retention obligations limited to what is necessary.
  • Unity Authentication: account data is retained until deleted through service mechanisms. Unity Lobby documents Player IDs in logs for up to 30 days. These periods must not be attributed automatically to Distributed Authority or Meta Anchors; those providers manage their data under applicable procedures.

Controls and rights

You can manage camera, microphone and tracking permissions on your headset; related features may stop working. Avoid unnecessary imports and select LAN mode when appropriate. These choices do not automatically erase existing data.

For access, correction, cancellation/deletion or objection (ARCO), withdrawing consent or limiting use/disclosure, email support_ss@fatyflyns.com with subject “SpellSurge Privacy”. Describe your request, a reply address and minimal details to locate data, such as alias, tournament or devices. Do not send passwords or tokens. We will explain proportionate identity/representative verification and any additional information needed.

Under applicable Mexican law, we will communicate our determination within 20 business days of receiving the request and, where granted, implement it within 15 business days after communicating the response. Legally permitted extensions or requests for information will be communicated. Each period may be extended once for an equal period when justified. Access may be provided electronically after identity verification. Your applicable rights and remedies before competent authorities remain available.

Deleting an editable map or resetting a tournament does not guarantee deletion of backups, labels, exports, copies on other devices or external service accounts. Small Basic is protected built-in content. For complete deletion, we review locations under our control and coordinate provider requests as needed. We do not have automatic remote access to users' devices or independent organizers' files. Uninstalling does not itself delete the Unity account.

Teenagers

SpellSurge is intended for users aged 13 or above, or a higher minimum required by their region/platform. It is not directed at children below that threshold. Teenagers must involve a parent or guardian where required. Where verified parental consent is necessary, the controller or organizer must arrange it before collecting or transmitting the minor's data; a written clause does not replace verification. Representatives can contact us to exercise rights or report unauthorized use. The app does not include automatic age or parenthood verification.

Changes

Updates and their date will appear on this page. Where required, we will communicate material changes and obtain necessary consent before applying new purposes. This notice does not limit mandatory rights.
